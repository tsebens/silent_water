# Negative prompt block

Seeded from `docs/art_direction.md`. Append to every concept prompt, or pass as the negative prompt where the provider supports one.

- sleek, smooth, rounded consumer-product shapes
- cyberpunk look, magenta-and-cyan neon, hard neon signage
- floodlighting, bright or saturated coloured light, lens flare
- large glass domes or big glazed walls
- ornament, decoration, gothic or art deco detail
- clean, brand-new, pristine surfaces; or sloppy, messy, random damage
- baked-in lighting, cast shadows or strong highlights in the subject
- text, logos, labels, watermarks, people
- cluttered background, multiple subjects, cropped subject
- bright, sunlit, tropical or cartoon ocean
- structures that read as rock, or rock that reads as a structure
