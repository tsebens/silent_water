Silent Water: a dark, deep-ocean world. Everything humans built feels industrial, utilitarian and competent: overbuilt, honest, worn by age and pressure but never sloppy. Nature is soft, irregular and organic. Low-poly forms with flat shading and strong, readable silhouettes; shape matters more than texture or detail.

Presentation: one subject, centred and fully in frame, on a plain neutral dark-grey background. Flat, even, frontal light with no cast shadows, no rim light and no dramatic lighting; the game adds lighting and fog. Three-quarter or straight-on view with minimal perspective distortion.
