# Style: canonical prompt block

Seeded from `docs/art_direction.md`. Prepend this block to every concept prompt; add the asset brief after it.

Brutalist concrete and steel, lit by soft fading neon, alone in a vast dark ocean. The feel is industrial, utilitarian and competent: massive, honest, overbuilt, worn by age and pressure but never sloppy.

Shapes: hard-edged, flat-faced and angular for anything built; soft, sloping and organic for terrain and living things. Heavy horizontal slabs, stepped and cantilevered tiers, deep overhangs with ribbed or coffered undersides, repeated vertical fins and ribs, thick columns and splayed struts that show the load path. Long ribbon windows and slots set deep in the mass; glass is a small recessed exception. Identical repeated bays and modules.

Materials: board-marked or poured concrete, steel frames, trusses and railings. Matte, muted surfaces with low-contrast wear. Little ornament; structure is the decoration.

Light: soft neon in orange, blue and green, used as thin lines, strips, slots and small point lights from the structure itself, never as floodlighting. Low intensity, wide glow, fast falloff into fog. No hard magenta-and-cyan cyberpunk signage. The surrounding ocean is deep teal to black with marine snow.

Presentation: single subject centred, neutral dark background, low-poly faceted forms with flat shading, orthographic-friendly framing, subject fully in frame.
