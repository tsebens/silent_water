## Style
A deep-sea creature shaped by the rift: plausible marine anatomy (deep-sea fish, lobe-finned coelacanth-like forms, cephalopods, isopods, eels) pushed slightly wrong, such as one feature repeated too many times, proportions off, eyes shrunken or absent in blind hunters. Measured dread, not gore. A bold silhouette readable at a distance: distinct head, fin and tail profile. Muted, desaturated skin in greys, bone, umber and dark teal; any bioluminescence is small flat-coloured spots or lines, not glow. Neutral swimming pose, side-on or three-quarter, fins and tentacles held clear of the body, mouth closed unless the design needs it.

## Avoid
- cute, cartoon or mascot features; anime eyes
- gore, exposed organs, blood
- humanoid features, dragons, fantasy monsters, armour plates or spikes for decoration
- wet glossy highlights, glowing aura
- concrete, machinery, architecture or any man-made object
