## Style
Raindrop's infrastructure in brutalist concrete and steel: massive, honest, overbuilt, built to outlast the people who made it. Heavy horizontal slabs, stepped and cantilevered tiers, deep overhangs with ribbed or coffered undersides, repeated vertical fins and ribs, thick columns and splayed struts that show the load path. Long ribbon windows and slots set deep in the mass; glass is a small recessed exception. Identical repeated bays and modules. Board-marked or poured concrete, steel frames, trusses and railings, with matte surfaces and low-contrast wear. Neon is thin lines, strips and slots in soft orange, blue or green, drawn as flat colour, not glow; emission is added in the engine.

## Avoid
- sleek, smooth, rounded shapes; large glass domes or glazed walls
- cyberpunk look, magenta-and-cyan neon, signage, floodlighting, glow and bloom
- ornament, gothic or art deco detail
- pristine new surfaces, or sloppy random damage
- structures that read as rock
