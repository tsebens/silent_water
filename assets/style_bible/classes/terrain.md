## Style
Seabed and geological features: rock outcrops, boulders, ridges, ledges, hydrothermal vent chimneys, sediment mounds. Soft, sloping, eroded and irregular, with layered strata and rounded weathering; nothing straight and nothing symmetrical. Muted dark greys, slate, umber and silt beige, with dull ochre or rust mineral crusts around vents. Always reads as natural rock, never as a built structure.

## Avoid
- straight edges, right angles, symmetry, repeated modules
- concrete, metal or any man-made object
- coral reefs, plants, seaweed, bright colours
- rock that reads as a building or ruin
