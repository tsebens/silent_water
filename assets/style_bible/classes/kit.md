## Style
Kit parts: the toggles, switches, rockers, lamps, bezels and levers that the sub's stations are built from. They are modelled in Onshape (see `assets/media/kit/README.md`), not generated, so this file only describes the intended look. Matte powder-coated grey bodies, safety-orange guards and levers, black grips and bezels, and a small LED indicator; chamfered edges, shallow knurling, shapes that read at arm's length.

## Avoid
- fillets, high-poly knurling or fine ridges
- chrome, gloss, legible text
