## Style
Equipment and props: compact, purposeful and heavy-duty, built from simple chunky parts with chamfered edges, with a function readable at a glance (valves, canisters, cases, panels, tools, pumps). Inside the sub: modern near-future working gear in matte painted metal and rubber, muted greys and gunmetal, with small safety-colour accents in orange or yellow; markings shown as plain blocks of colour, never legible text. Network hardware left outside on the seabed is angular, bolted and weathered, built like the stations.

## Avoid
- sleek consumer-product shapes, chrome, high gloss
- screens showing content, legible text or numbers
- fine greebles, tiny detail, cables everywhere
- organic or rock-like shapes
